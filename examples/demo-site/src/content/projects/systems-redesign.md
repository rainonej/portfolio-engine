## The challenge

Five product teams were building independently, resulting in inconsistent UX, duplicated effort, and inaccessible components that couldn't meet WCAG 2.1 AA requirements.

## Approach

We audited every component across all products, identified convergence points, and built a token-based system that could flex to each team's needs while enforcing shared constraints. Engineering and design collaborated from day one.

## Outcome

The system now serves 200+ designers and engineers. New features ship 30% faster. Accessibility audits that once required weeks of remediation now pass on first review.
