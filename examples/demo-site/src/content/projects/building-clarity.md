## The challenge

The existing portal had grown organically over a decade, accumulating layers of bureaucratic logic that left users confused and frustrated. Over 60% of sessions ended without completion.

## Approach

We began with six weeks of contextual inquiry — sitting with people as they tried to navigate their benefits, watching where they stalled, what words they used, what they expected. From there we built a clear mental model of the task space and redesigned from the ground up.

## Outcome

After launch, task completion rose from 38% to 78%. Support call volume dropped by 52% in the first quarter. Most importantly, the people using it told us it finally felt like it was built for them.
