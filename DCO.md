# Developer Certificate of Origin

Portfolio Engine uses a Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO)-style contribution policy.

By contributing to this project, you certify that you have the right to submit the contribution under the project license.

## Certificate

By making a contribution to this project, I certify that:

1. The contribution was created in whole or in part by me and I have the right to submit it under the open-source license indicated in this repository; or

2. The contribution is based upon previous work that, to the best of my knowledge, is covered under an appropriate open-source license and I have the right under that license to submit that work with modifications under the same open-source license; or

3. The contribution was provided directly to me by another person who certified conditions 1 or 2, and I have not modified it.

I also certify that I understand and agree that this project and the contribution are public and that a record of the contribution, including my sign-off, may be maintained indefinitely and redistributed consistent with this project or the open-source license involved.

## Sign-off

Contributors should sign off commits when requested:

```text
Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
```

You can add this automatically with:

```bash
git commit -s
```

## AI-assisted contributions

If an AI tool helped create a contribution, the human contributor remains responsible for the contribution and its sign-off.

Do not sign off on AI-generated work you do not understand or do not have the right to submit.
