# Code of Conduct

Portfolio Engine is a small maintainer-led open-source project. Contributors and participants are expected to keep discussion respectful, constructive, and focused.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction.
- Keep criticism specific and actionable.
- Respect project boundaries and maintainer decisions.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Be clear when using AI-generated or AI-assisted content.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory comments, sexualized language or imagery, personal attacks, repeated off-topic disruption, doxxing, deliberately submitting secrets/private data/harmful code, and misrepresenting AI-generated work as independently reviewed human work.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close issues or PRs, block users, refuse contributions, or restrict participation.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub or the contact listed in `SECURITY.md` if the issue is sensitive.
